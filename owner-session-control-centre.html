⏱️ Global administrator inactivity timeout

This setting applies to every administrator. Real pointer, keyboard, touch, scroll or form activity resets the inactivity clock. Background heartbeat checks keep a live browser lock fresh but do not count as user activity.

Session warning: one minute before expiry, administrators receive a live countdown with Continue Session and Log Out Now actions. A short 90-second network recovery grace period is allowed for temporary connection loss.
