Submit a Video or GIF Advertisement

Upload an MP4 video or animated GIF for PARROTHIT Admin review. After approval, Admin can allocate it to either of the two video/GIF advertising cards on a webpage.

Payment verification is not required for submitting, approving or allocating a video/GIF advertisement. The creative must still pass Admin review before appearing on the website.

New advertiser? Create a free video/GIF account

You do not need to purchase an image-ad plan or verify a payment to create this account. A Customer ID and private 8-digit PIN are required so you can track your video/GIF submission.

Existing advertiser? Use your Customer ID and tracking PIN

Use the Customer ID and private 8-digit tracking PIN created when you applied for PARROTHIT advertising. Your PIN is checked securely and is not stored on this page.
