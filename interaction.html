🦜 Community guidelines & terms

Before entering Parrots interaction, please review and accept these rules:

  1. Respect others: no harassment, threats, hate speech, bullying or targeted abuse.
  2. No harmful or illegal content: do not post violent threats, scams, impersonation, unlawful material or content that exploits children.
  3. Protect privacy: do not publish another person's private information, private conversations or images without permission.
  4. Share responsibly: label opinions clearly, avoid spreading unverified claims as facts, and respect copyright.
  5. Pictures and voice: only upload media you have the right to share. Do not include sensitive personal details.
  6. Moderation: posts may be hidden or removed for guideline violations. Access may be restricted to protect the community.
  7. Personal responsibility: be thoughtful; interactions are between users and should not be treated as professional advice.

These community rules are a practical usage notice, not a substitute for legal advice or a complete legal privacy policy.
