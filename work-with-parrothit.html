What your CV should include

  1. Professional profile: a concise summary of your experience and career focus.
  2. Personal contact: full name, professional email, phone and city/country.
  3. Education: qualification, course, institution and dates.
  4. Work experience: most recent role first, employer, dates, duties and measurable results.
  5. Core skills: relevant technical, editorial, communication or operational skills.
  6. Certifications: verified professional training and certificates.
  7. Portfolio: selected work samples or professional links where relevant.
  8. Referees: two professional referees, with their permission, if available.
CV tip: Use clear headings, consistent dates, a simple font and reverse chronological order. Avoid false claims, unnecessary personal details and decorative layouts that make your CV difficult to read.

Application process

1. Select an advertised role.
2. Complete the form and upload both images.
3. Admin 1 (Owner) reviews your application.
4. If accepted, you receive an official employment letter, job description and secure administrator account setup instructions by email.

Privacy: submitted documents are kept in a private storage bucket. They are not publicly indexed or linked from the visitor website.

Location references: country and administrative-area options are loaded from the Open Admin Data static directory where available, with a secondary directory and manual-entry fallback. Nigeria administrative divisions are provided under CC BY 4.0. Street and house numbers are entered manually because public street directories are not complete across all countries.
