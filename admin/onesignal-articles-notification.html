How these settings work

Saved settings apply to the next article notifications sent by PARROTHIT. When the site is open, the public foreground alert now mirrors the selected layout, including the full-width Big Picture image. When the site is closed, OneSignal sends the matching platform image fields; the phone/browser controls how much of the image appears while the notification is collapsed.

Action rule: every PARROTHIT article notification has exactly one action — Read full article. PARROTHIT does not add an unsubscribe action to article notifications.

PARROTHIT recommendation

Use Big Picture for most published stories. Keep Breaking News Spotlight for genuinely urgent reports marked as breaking. Use a clear, public HTTPS article image and a short headline so the alert is easy to scan.

02 · NOTIFICATION COLOURS

Choose multiple colours

Choose one or more accent colours. PARROTHIT selects one of your chosen colours for each future send.

Platform note: OneSignal's Android accent colour affects the small notification icon/accent area on supported Android native notifications. Browsers control their own notification background and cannot be forced to use a custom colour by a website. Your choices are still saved and supplied to supported platforms.
