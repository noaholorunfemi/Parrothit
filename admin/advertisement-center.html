5. Payment configuration

Bank transfer details are shown to customers. Gateway checkouts use secure Supabase Edge Function secrets; do not enter secret keys here or in website JavaScript.

Supported options are manual bank transfer, Paystack and Flutterwave hosted checkout. USSD and gateway bank transfer appear only when enabled and supported by your merchant account. Card checkout is also available through the gateways.
