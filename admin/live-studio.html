5. Multi-platform distribution & production notes

For multiple destinations, connect this WHIP output to a streaming provider that supports restreaming/relay, or use OBS Studio with the provider's RTMP/WHIP details. PARROTHIT does not expose or store RTMP stream keys in its public settings.

Browser multi-camera support here means selecting/switching between available camera devices. Simultaneous multi-camera mixing, graphics overlays, remote guests, server-side recording and automatic multi-platform relay require a dedicated production/streaming service.
