🧰 Destination coverage & safeguards

Recommended checks before a permanent removal.

Category news feeds

Published posts are automatically included in their matching category page based on the post's category_id. Changing the category in the editor moves the article to the newly selected category feed.

Category card slots

The manager checks category_card_assignments and site_settings.category_cards, including original cards and additional category-card positions.

Homepage destinations

Homepage News Card 1–15 assignments are detected from site_settings.homepage_cards. Existing card positions are displayed beside each matching article.

Featured story destinations

Featured Story Card 1–16 records are detected from site_settings.news_cards. Article IDs and article slug destinations are both used where available.
