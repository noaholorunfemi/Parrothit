⚠️ Before you continue

You are entering PARROTHIT's highest-risk editorial control page. Changes here affect articles that are already live on the public website.

  • Editing: saving can immediately overwrite the published headline, summary, article body, author, breaking-news flag, or featured image and synchronise copied public-card content.
  • Deleting: permanent deletion removes the published post and clears mapped public destinations. Related comments, likes, and newsroom references may also be removed by database cascade rules.
  • Recovery: deletion cannot be undone from this page. A backup may be the only way to reconstruct lost content.
  • Verification: check the headline, article ID, public destinations, and content carefully before every save or deletion.
Recommended: export a filtered CSV before editorial clean-up and keep a separate backup of the original article content and images.

🧰 Destination coverage & safeguards

Recommended checks before a permanent removal.

Category news feeds

Published posts are automatically included in their matching category page based on the post's category_id. Changing the category in the editor moves the article to the newly selected category feed.

Category card slots

The manager checks category_card_assignments and site_settings.category_cards, including original cards and additional category-card positions.

Homepage destinations

Homepage News Card 1–15 assignments are detected from site_settings.homepage_cards. Existing card positions are displayed beside each matching article.

Featured story destinations

Featured Story Card 1–16 records are detected from site_settings.news_cards. Article IDs and article slug destinations are both used where available.
