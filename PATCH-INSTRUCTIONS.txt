PARROTHIT NEWS CARD DISPLAY PATCH

This patch updates the public news-card presentation only.

Changes:
- News cards show the news image (when one is available) and headline only.
- Category labels, summaries, descriptions, author/date metadata, and read-more buttons are hidden.
- The headline itself links to the full article detail destination.
- Clicking elsewhere on the card does not navigate.
- The card script cache version is updated in the included public pages.

Install:
1. Back up your current project.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy the included Parrothit-main folder contents into the matching folder in your existing project, allowing overwrite.
4. Commit and deploy.

No database migration is required. This patch assumes the previous headline/image card patch is already installed.
